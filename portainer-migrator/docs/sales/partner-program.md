# Partner Program

## Overview

The Portainer Migrator Partner Program enables consultants, agencies, and MSPs to offer migration services to their clients while earning revenue.

## Partner Tiers

### Referral Partner
*Entry level - no commitment*

**Benefits**:
- 15% commission on referrals
- Partner badge
- Marketing materials

**Requirements**:
- Sign partner agreement
- Introduce via warm email

### Certified Partner
*Active sellers*

**Benefits**:
- 25% commission
- Deal registration protection
- Co-marketing opportunities
- Partner portal access
- Technical training

**Requirements**:
- Complete certification
- 3+ deals per quarter
- Customer support capability

### Premier Partner
*Strategic relationship*

**Benefits**:
- 35% commission
- White-label options
- Custom pricing
- Joint marketing budget
- Dedicated partner manager
- Roadmap input

**Requirements**:
- 10+ deals per quarter
- Certified team members
- Annual commitment

## Commission Structure

| Partner Tier | Year 1 | Renewals |
|--------------|--------|----------|
| Referral | 15% | 10% |
| Certified | 25% | 15% |
| Premier | 35% | 20% |

*Commission paid on license revenue, not services*

## Deal Registration

### Process
1. Register deal via partner portal
2. 24-hour validation
3. 90-day protection period
4. Commission paid on close

### Protected Deals
- No direct sales competition
- Exclusive territory (if applicable)
- Renewal rights maintained

## FAQ

**Q: Can I resell at my own price?**
A: Certified and Premier partners can add services. License pricing is fixed unless white-labeled.

**Q: Do I need to provide support?**
A: Referral partners do not. Certified partners handle L1, we handle L2/L3.

**Q: Can I use my own branding?**
A: Premier partners have white-label options available.

**Q: What's the payment schedule?**
A: Monthly payments, net-30 from customer payment.
