## ROI Calculator {#calculator}

### Time Savings

| Manual Migration | Migrator | Savings |
|-----------------|----------|---------|
| 4-8 hours (10 stacks) | 15 min | 90%+ |
| 20-40 hours (50 stacks) | 1 hour | 95%+ |
| 50-100 hours (100+ stacks) | 2 hours | 97%+ |

### Cost Calculation

**Manual Migration Cost**:
```
DevOps Engineer: $100/hour
50 stacks × 30 min/stack = 25 hours
25 hours × $100 = $2,500
```

**Portainer Migrator Cost**:
```
Professional License: $299/year
Migration Time: ~1 hour
Total: $299 + $100 = $399
```

**Savings**: $2,101 (84%)

## FAQ

**Q: Can I upgrade mid-year?**
A: Yes, pay the difference prorated.

**Q: What happens when my license expires?**
A: Existing migrations work, but no new migrations or updates.

**Q: Do I need a license per server?**
A: Standard/Professional: 1 instance. Enterprise: unlimited.

**Q: Is there a monthly option?**
A: Not currently, annual only.
